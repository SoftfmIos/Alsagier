Alsagier Privacy Policy

Alsagier by Softfm

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Softfm respects your privacy. Alsagier is a personal productivity and daily planning application designed to help organize your calendar, projects, tasks, habits, prayer times, and daily schedule.

Information Stored by Alsagier

Alsagier stores information that you create in the app, such as projects, tasks, habits, preferences, and daily schedules locally on your device. The current version of Alsagier does not require you to create an account.

Calendar Access

If you grant permission, Alsagier accesses calendar events on your device to include existing appointments and commitments in your daily schedule. Calendar information is used for scheduling within the app. Alsagier does not modify or delete your existing calendar events.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Alsagier may create local notifications to remind you about scheduled activities and items in your daily plan.

Prayer Times

Alsagier uses prayer-time information to help protect prayer periods when organizing your daily schedule.

Live Activities

Alsagier may use Apple's Live Activities feature to display information about your current and upcoming schedule on supported iPhone Lock Screens and Dynamic Island.

Data Sharing

Softfm does not sell your personal information. The current version of Alsagier does not transmit your projects, tasks, habits, or daily planning information to Softfm servers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Alsagier's planning data is stored locally on your device. You can remove Alsagier data using the reset functionality provided within the app. Deleting the app from your device also removes locally stored Alsagier data, subject to Apple's device and backup functionality.

Your Choices

You can manage permissions such as Calendar and Notifications through iOS Settings. You may revoke these permissions at any time.

Children's Privacy

Alsagier is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Alsagier's features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
